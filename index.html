How this works
  • Each day you get your due reviews first, then new problems up to your daily goal.
  • The pattern is hidden. Pick what you think it is before you code. That's the skill interviews test.
  • Solved cold means no hints and no peeking. It comes back in 3, then 7, then 14 days. Three cold solves in a row and it counts as locked in.
  • Needed hints comes back in 2 days. Couldn't solve comes back tomorrow and resets.
  • Be honest with the buttons. Rating a peek as "cold" only fools you.
